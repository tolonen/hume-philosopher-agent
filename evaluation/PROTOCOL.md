# Agent evaluation protocol, v0.1

HPA-001 and HPA-002 are **development/comparison cases**. They cannot be relabeled
as untouched holdouts after their arguments have shaped the method. A known
objection reproduced independently is a methodological result, not original work.

No evaluation holdout has yet been constituted. Before choosing one, freeze its
claim inventory, source access, selection rule and permitted context. Choose
argument families or publications separate from those used to develop the method;
do not use thin paragraph splits of the same dispute as evidence of independence.
Record contamination risks and actual source exposure. Pretraining exposure is
unknown; no claim of a model having never seen a dispute is warranted.

Assess citation accuracy, reconstruction fidelity, logical consequences, strongest
reply handling, revision honesty, alternative explanatory reach, novelty coverage
and consequentiality separately. These are review dimensions, not numerical
confidence scores or automatic certification. Preserve failed objections.

Current assessments are automated and same-session configured agents. They are
separate argumentative checks, not independent historical witnesses or human
endorsement. Exact model deployment, sampling parameters and reasoning effort
are not exposed; record null rather than inferred values. No theorem prover or
neural computation was used in this stage.
