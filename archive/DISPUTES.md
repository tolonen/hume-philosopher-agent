## Positive resolution developed from HPA-003

[HPA-006](dossiers/HPA-006.md) supplies the missing inferential account: causal
commitment and correction without determining substance. T1.4.5.30/.33 separates
those questions; EHU11.25 distinguishes independent kind evidence from an
unsupported additional prediction; D4.14 permits ultimate explanatory ignorance.
T1.3.16 and Book2/3 test the account against other minds and misleading sympathy.
Result: **contestable positive reconstruction** supporting restricted suspension
against science-only exclusion, while conceding ordinary bodily realism. Actual
suspension and global anti-perspectivalism remain unproved. Kim2019 and Inoue2003
are full-read precedents; integration novelty unestablished. Full Waldow comparison
and decisive page collation are the next discriminants.

## Constitution and assent: Hakkarainen–Baxter integration

[HPA-007](dossiers/HPA-007.md) now includes Baxter's direct answer to Hakkarainen2012
in the complete substance manuscript, MS25–26. Genuine complex unity versus related
plurality remains a substantive disagreement. Hakkarainen2015 spatial properties is
fully assessed; Hakkarainen2019 unity remains unassessed. The mass and real compound
passages were already anticipated by Baxter and are not new refutations.

Root fully read Baxter's assent argument: causal-rule authority is treated as
ultimately practical (MS17–18), while theoretical/normative content does not establish
active endorsement (MS20). This directly limits HPA006's use of positive assertions.
His exact disagreement with Garrett is recorded, but the full1997/2007 Garrett
arguments remain an additional reading requirement. The integrated positive account
separates causal evidence, constitution, persistence and assent. No resolution of
the Appendix or complete reconciliation of the rival ontologies is claimed.

## Prioritized new original-reading targets

1. **Composition realism versus nihilism:** Hakkarainen 2019, *Hume on the Unity of Determinations of Extension*,219–233, and the complete Baxter argument it opposes. [Publication record](https://researchportal.tuni.fi/en/publications/hume-on-the-unity-of-determinations-of-extension/). Present access is discovery/abstract only. T1.2.3 and the individuation of an ordered whole provide the exact next premise for HPA-005. Do not infer Baxter's complete position from Hakkarainen's description.
2. **Competing repairs of complex ideas:** Qu 2022, *Complex Ideas and Hume's Separability Principle*,517–534, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/mind/fzaa099). Full original acquisition unresolved. Clay's original establishes his own adopted definition and credits Qu's overlap example; it is not access to Qu's full comparison of alternatives.
3. **Personal identity and the Appendix:** Garrett's *Rethinking Hume's Second Thoughts about Personal Identity* and his exchange with Kail; Cottrell's *Minds, Composition, and Hume's Appendix*. Original full arguments unassessed. Test whether the Appendix's problem concerns unity, awareness of unity, or explanatory connection, keeping1739 and1740 witnesses separate. This is a real adjacent metaphysical dispute, not another separability dossier counted twice.
4. **Representation and Book 2:** Cottrell's2021 *Representation and Copying in Hume's Treatise*, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3998/ergo.1141), alongside Demeter's account and Hume's sympathy argument. Full Cottrell original unassessed. Test how an idea of another's passion can represent it before becoming the observer's impression; distinguish functional role from intrinsic resemblance.

The initial eight areas remain covered: causal powers and liberty through HPA-001; induction through HPA-004; representation and belief through the connected claims; scepticism and external objects through HPA-003; identity through the next targets; motivation through HPA-002. Metaphysics proper is now an additional substantial branch through HPA-005 and composition. A binary is useful only when its alternatives disagree on a specified claim; several of these tests show why familiar labels cross-cut rather than settle that claim.

New work does not reopen a closed objection without new evidence. No new source is treated as training data, no schedule is implied, and inaccessible originals remain unassessed.
