# Reconstruction cycle v5 — prospective design

12 October 2026. User brief: systematic reconstruction and dependency testing across works and textual stages. The preceding seven dossiers are provisional. This design precedes new stage-five retrieval; the earlier corpus and passages have already been exposed to the investigators. It is not a blinded holdout.

## Central question

What conditions distinguish a permissible causal attribution from an illicit projection of existence, unity or authority, and did Hume change those conditions after the Treatise? Can one account explain bodies, other people, theological inference and empirical reliance without deriving every ontological or epistemic conclusion from success?

## Rival models and commitments to test

M1 — Foundational scientific realism: successful warranted human science supplies independently existing bodies/persons, complex unities and rational endorsement. Missing bridges: success→warrant; other-person content→human-perception independence; organized interaction→one being; affirmative content→endorsement. Strong versions must supply these bridges rather than simply assume them.

M2 — Universal suspension/fiction: metaphysical uncertainty blocks genuinely warranted unobserved-object claims; constructive statements operate as useful suppositions. Test whether this predicts more suspension than Hume articulates, and whether it can explain corrective empirical comparisons without changing their subjects or taking normative language as automatic endorsement.

M3 — Evidence-calibrated causal projection: learned relations and independent relevant experience constrain extension to unobserved instances; identity and constitution may be needed to formulate a test without themselves being proved by its success. This predicts genuine but defeasible attribution, distinct limits on attributes and consequences, and a religious case that differs by evidence rather than topic alone. This model requires its own theory of empirical authority. It must face external-world circularity and Baxter's passive-assent account; not merely declare compatibility with them.

M4 — Developmental revision: early Treatise reasoning promises authority/unity it cannot sustain; later works retract or replace the premises while retaining practical conclusions. Reconstruct the early dependencies first. Predict identifiable changed premises or new arguments, not omission alone. M4 competes with continuity plus revised presentation.

## Consequences fixed before further search

C1. If M1 infers perception-independent objects from external signs, its warrant must distinguish real external objects from the perceptions supplied as signs. Test T1.4.2 and EHU12; successful prediction alone cannot establish the conjunction it needs.

C2. If M2 excludes all inferences to unobserved minds, ordinary testimony and animal inference should be unavailable or merely instrumental. Test their actual functions, not isolated existence vocabulary.

C3. If M3 makes familiar causal kinds decisive, a novel instance of an independently familiar kind should not be excluded merely because it is novel. Test novel voice, divine minimal analogy and testimony for unusual events. A theological subject-matter exception would need independent motivation.

C4. If causal interdependence constitutes one being or consciousness, causal connections between people threaten the individuation rule. Test sympathy, changing organized objects and the Appendix; add a discriminating organization premise only if text motivates it.

C5. If empirical success establishes rational endorsement, strong correctable passive belief should be impossible. Test Baxter's exact original account and Hume's regress/recovery sequence. Normative content alone will not be counted as defeat.

C6. If the Treatise's introspective psychology supplies uniquely sufficient inferential authority, historical explanation or testimony without first-person access should either acquire the same authority by a stated bridge or fall outside it. Search history, essays and correspondence for calibration practices and their limits. Genre transfer needs a reconstructed argument, not matching words.

C7. If later omission indicates doctrinal abandonment, find independent evidence of withdrawal or an altered dependency. The Appendix's explicit dissatisfaction is a positive control; silence in the Enquiry is not equivalent evidence.

## Records to build during investigation

Propositions with source/state/voice/scope and premise origin; conjunctive dependency edges; competing models; chronology and revision hypotheses; consequence tests fixed before observation; actual indexed/searched/context-read/reconstructed coverage. Preserve contrary outcomes and failed models. Deliver a sustained argument with a consequential conclusion, strongest objection and response, or explain exactly which central proposal failed.
