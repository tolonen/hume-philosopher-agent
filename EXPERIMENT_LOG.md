# Stages, corrections and remaining claims

All four substantive stages occurred on 12 October 2026. This log is an edited retrospective account drawn from dossiers and stage ledgers. It is not a complete transcript or an exhaustive record of every retrieval and prompt. The public repository starts after these investigations; the private development history is not represented as public Git history.

## Stage 1: two exact claims and two withdrawn objections

The brief required original scholarship and a primary evidence chain. The assistant established the searchable witness, selected Millican on causal semantics and Zimmerman on the motivational argument, and sought separate automated defences.

- Millican objection: uniform legitimate use need not logically imply reduction. Defeating limitation: this alone did not preserve Hume's stronger assertions; Beebee and Millican already discuss the representational possibility. Verdict: withdraw proposed refutation.
- Zimmerman objection: the representation argument might concern contradiction without independent motivational force. Defeating evidence: T3.1.1's retrospective attribution of motivational function and Zimmerman's own use of it. Verdict: withdraw the exclusive objection; retain a limited, unpromoted adequacy question.

The [first report](reports/first-investigations.md) is retained as historical evidence, including its then-unresolved Zimmerman download problem. That source was subsequently preserved; a historical access failure is not a current reading gap.

## Stage 2: user-directed deepening

The user requested more substantial work around Garrett, Hakkarainen and metaphysics. Three further dossiers resulted.

- Garrett: complete dialogue and later reply showed that the easy objections ignored concessions about fallibility and reflection. Qualified support survived; faulty demonstration remains disputed.
- Separability: the local point/whole distinction survived. The overlap test first looked promising as an additional insight, but was identified as already published by Qu and discussed by Clay. Novelty suggestion withdrawn.
- Science and scepticism: constructive science was shown compatible with leaving an ultimate-origin question open. The stronger inference to actual suspension or epistemic warrant was withheld. Other-person attribution became the next discriminating problem.

See [the second report](reports/second-investigations.md), HPA-003–005 and the stage-two assessment notes.

## Stage 3: positive reconstruction requested

The user explicitly asked for new evidence from Hume and a positive solution, rather than criticism alone. The assistant combined complete T1.4.5 and EHU11 reading with further primary investigations and an adversarial assessment of other minds.

- Early possibility: privilege mental causes while excluding material causes. Withdrawn after T1.4.5.30's affirmative bodily-causation evidence.
- Early possibility: treat all relations as known while intrinsic properties remain unknown. Narrowed by T1.4.5's distinction between causal/time relations and spatial conjunction.
- Positive proposal: independently familiar causal types permit limited analogical inference; an isolated effect does not license unlimited enrichment of an unfamiliar cause.
- Novelty correction: own-case analogy already occurs in Kim and is considered by Inoue. It is not a discovery.
- Remaining limits: local inquiry does not establish metaphysical suspension, eliminate ordinary realism, answer global scepticism or prove a new solution to other minds.

See HPA-006 and the stage-three notes. The main positive report now includes stage-four qualifications, rather than presenting the earlier version as final.

## Stage 4: Hakkarainen and Baxter integrated

The user asked to add Hakkarainen and Baxter to the positive account. Full accessible originals on spatial properties, substance, assent and a précis were read across investigators; original-source gaps were retained.

- Proposed reconciliation by distinguishing spatial unity from temporal identity: rejected as complete reconciliation, because Baxter also contests simultaneous complex unity and directly replies to Hakkarainen2012.
- Proposed use of stable character: restricted to attribution within a tracked history. T1.4.6.19 permits character change, so stable character is neither necessary nor sufficient for identity.
- Proposed causal solution of mental unity: withheld. The Appendix remains unsolved, and causal connections extend between people.
- Proposed refutation of passive assent by positive/normative language: rejected. Baxter's account expressly permits such content without active endorsement.

The surviving integration distinguishes causal evidence, constitution, persistence and assent. It is contestable and not established as original. See HPA-007 and the amended positive report. The unity/identity distinction and familiar countertexts already have scholarly precedents.
