## HPA-001: New Hume, semantic exhaustiveness and liberty

[Dossier and complete evidence chain](../dossiers/HPA-001.md).

**Target:** Millican 2009, p.699, argues that the definitions' argumentative role
requires a semantic, rather than merely epistemological, interpretation. The
target concerns what Hume's argument requires; it is not an accusation that
Millican mistakes a summary for a text or merely assumes observation is ontology.

**Attempted objection:** warranted causal attribution and one uniform causal
concept do not, as a matter of logic, imply exhaustive reduction of their referent.
T 1.4.5.33's qualification about our notion and T 1.3.14.27's unknown qualities
appear to make a non-reductive alternative possible. The religious defence in
T 2.3.2.4 can also survive a concession about another material connexion.

**Defence:** Hume does more than establish evidential parity. T 1.3.14.33 denies
a foundation in nature for the physical/moral distinction; T 2.3.1.4 makes the
two particulars essential; A 34 demands another idea and says the preceding
reasoning prevents it. Unknown qualities are not thereby another intelligible
causal necessity. A fallback defence of religious innocence does not retract
the prior doctrinal proof. Millican already distinguishes pragmatic sufficiency
from semantic determination at pp.690–692.

**Response and verdict:** the alternative would need a Humean representation
theory that allows wider reference while excluding the very physical/moral
subtypes Hume rejects. Logical compatibility alone does not establish that
theory. Millican 2011, pp.141–142, conditionally acknowledges such reconciliation
and locates the objection in the representational account. Thus we must neither
describe him as overlooking the possibility nor claim his liberty argument
refutes every imaginable realist reconstruction. **The proposed refutation is
withdrawn; a contestable alternative survives.**

**Positive alternative and limits:** Hume enforces one legitimate causal concept
and application policy across bodies and minds, denying the opponent a second
intelligible necessity merely by renaming an unknown quality. That explains the
qualifications and the challenge for another idea. It has not yet explained the
unqualified nature claim well enough to defeat Millican's reading. The exact
downstream question is whether liberty/necessity independently settles causal
metaphysics; this does not collapse the entire Old Hume interpretation.

**Novelty:** Beebee 2007, especially pp.425–430, already develops the relevant
family of arguments; Millican's 2009 note61 and 2011 §IV reply directly. This is
an established dispute reproduced and examined, not an original discovery.
The [separate defence](../notes/adversarial-HPA-001.md) identifies the same
representation step as the alternative's weakness, from independent source
inspection. Its agreement is not historical evidence.

## HPA-002: Book 2, representation and motivational impotence

[Dossier and complete evidence chain](../dossiers/HPA-002.md).

**Target:** Zimmerman 2007, p.219, says the representation argument must establish
reason's independent inability to cause action. He distinguishes normative
criticism from causal influence and permits mediated influence; the target must
retain his qualifications and his functional reconstruction.

**Attempted objection:** T 2.3.3.5's explanation of contradiction might establish
only a category distinction between passion and truth-apt representation, not
an independent causal proof. The objection concerned argumentative function,
not whether Hume held the motivational thesis.

**Defence:** T 3.1.1.8 explicitly recalls an inertness argument; .10 assigns it an
indirect motivational use, while .12 and .16 explain mediated causation. This is
Hume's own retrospective account in the next Treatise volume, not a much later
Enquiry imported without chronology. Zimmerman cites this evidence on the target
page. The argument's local contradiction machinery can serve that broader purpose.

**Response and verdict:** the proposed exclusive division between truth-apt
contradiction and causal purpose is untenable against this evidence. **The
initial objection is withdrawn.** A weaker question about whether functional
classification proves incapacity remains unpromoted. Insufficiency does not
logically entail impossibility, but Zimmerman supplies the stronger function-
delimiting premise and substantive psychology. Finding a weak reformulation
does not refute that complete argument.

**Positive reading:** reason cannot disprove a bare passion as a false
representation; it can correct associated beliefs and thereby redirect or remove
dependent motivation. Representation confirms a view drawing on the surrounding
psychology. This accounts for the examples and Book3 retrospective account without
uniquely establishing modern functionalism. No refutation of Zimmerman's wider
non-instrumental-reasons interpretation follows. The [separate defence](../notes/adversarial-HPA-002.md)
states its selective access and nonblinded status explicitly.

**Novelty:** exact-title, author and related-literature searches did not yield a
fully inspected direct reply. That is limited coverage, not proof of originality.
Radcliffe/McCarty 2018 and Cohon/Owen 1997 are justified next original sources;
their arguments were not assessed from search results.

## Strongest surviving result and agent evaluation

The strongest result is a **bounded defence of the targeted interpretations
against our attempted objections**, with known competing readings and their
remaining burdens stated explicitly. There is no finding to promote as a
consequential scholarly error. Two important diagnostic distinctions emerged:
uniform use does not itself establish reduction, and an argument's logical
adequacy is distinct from its intended motivational function. Neither distinction
is claimed as new scholarship.

The versioned record preserves initial hypotheses, defeating evidence and reasons
for withdrawal. Assessments are automated, not expert endorsement. Exact model
deployment and sampling configuration were not exposed; the record says so.
Prior training exposure is unknown. These cases developed the method and cannot
serve as untouched evaluation holdouts. No uncontaminated novelty benchmark or
human validation result has been claimed.

Decisive scholarly quotations were checked in page-associated PDF extraction.
Page-image verification failed. Primary wording was checked in the public edited
witness; a private preserved witness was independently queried for a relevant
variant, but complete critical/facsimile collation remains unfinished. These
limitations matter before promoting an intervention.
