## Citation conventions and independent checking

`T` refers to the *Treatise*, normally book.part.section.paragraph; `A` to the
*Abstract*; `E` or `EHU` to the *Enquiry concerning Human Understanding*;
`D` to the *Dialogues concerning Natural Religion*. `SBN` and `KS` retain the
pagination labels used by the linked public transcriptions. `MS` means the
specific accessible author manuscript, whose pagination may differ from the
published version. A whole-section link identifies the textual sequence; consult
the cited paragraph within it.

The bibliography links to accessible originals or publication records.
Reading ledgers report what an automated investigator inspected; a hash proves
the identity of an audited file, not the truth of an interpretation. Where the
source itself is private, a public reader must check the corresponding passage
through a lawful independent edition rather than treating the receipt as a
replacement for the text.
