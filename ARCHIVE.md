# Full research record

The material below supports the short argument in the review text. It preserves earlier claims, failed proposals, corrections and technical records. It is optional for the initial three-question assessment.

| Material | Location | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Detailed current argument | [Integrated reconstruction](archive/reports/integrated-reconstruction-v5.md) | Full treatment of causation, objects, persons, composition, assent and chronology. |
| Experiment account | [Aims, method and results](archive/EXPERIMENT.md) | Explains what was attempted, done and left unresolved. |
| Research history | [Revision log](archive/EXPERIMENT_LOG.md) and [earlier reports](archive/reports) | Records failed proposals and their correction. |
| Individual debates | [Seven dossiers](archive/dossiers) | Earlier investigations of Millican, Zimmerman, Garrett, Hakkarainen, Baxter and others. These remain provisional. |
| Source evidence | [Sources](archive/SOURCES.md), [bibliography](archive/bibliography.bib) and [reading notes](archive/notes) | Distinguishes what was read from what remains unassessed. |
| Formal and evaluation records | [Models](archive/models), [checks](archive/scripts) and [evaluation](archive/evaluation) | Makes premises, countermodels and automated assessments inspectable. |
| Instructions | [Original brief](archive/REQUEST.md) and [reconstruction brief](archive/REQUEST-v5.md) | Preserves the assigned tasks. |

The [previous landing page](archive/README-previous.md) and [previous review guide](archive/REVIEW_GUIDE-previous.md) are retained as part of the experiment's history. Their multiple reading routes have been replaced by the short review text.

Paths inside the archived research records generally refer to the archive directory. To run the optional formal checker from the repository root:

```sh
python3 -B archive/scripts/check_reconstruction_v5.py
```

The checker tests stipulated formal consequences, not historical fidelity. [The current file manifest](PUBLICATION_MANIFEST.json) records paths and checksums. Full scholarly articles, licensed corpus files and conversation exports are not included.
